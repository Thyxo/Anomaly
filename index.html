Residential night watch.

Duty instructions
  1. Each camera shows a room in your home. Learn what it normally looks like — the start of the shift is quiet.
  2. Something enters through one of the rooms and moves one room closer to you at a time.
  3. When a room looks wrong, press REPORT and mark the wrong thing on the image.
  4. Nothing changes while you are looking at it. Changes happen on the feeds you are not watching.
  5. Listen. Sometimes you can hear that something has arrived.
  6. False reports, and watching one camera for too long, raise interference. At 100% every feed drops.
  7. If it reaches your room, the shift is over.

Keys: 1–9 cameras · R report · Esc cancel · F fullscreen

Night shift

Add-ons

Add-ons change how a shift plays and work in every mode. Each one gives you something new to decide.

House setup

Privacy. Photos are stored only in this browser (IndexedDB) on this device. They are sent to an AI service only if you press “Calibrate cameras” with AI generation (Mode A) enabled. Manual anomaly images (Mode B) and the procedural fallback never leave the device.

Add one photo per room, taken from where a security camera would sit (high corner, wide angle, lights as they are at night). Mark the room you will be sitting in while playing.

Which rooms connect directly to each other? The anomaly walks this graph toward your room, one room per step.

Mode A — AI generated

Sends each room photo plus a varied prompt to an image-editing model and stores the result. The changed area is located by comparing before/after.

Mode B — Upload your own anomaly images

Edit a room photo in ChatGPT or Gemini, then upload the result here and mark where the anomaly is.

Prompt helper — ready-to-copy prompts

Open ChatGPT or Gemini, attach one of your room photos, paste a prompt, download the result and upload it above. Pick the original photo it was made from so the game can suggest the area automatically.

Library

The export holds your photos, every AI and manual anomaly image, the layout and the marked areas. Import it later (or on another device) to reuse everything without generating again.
